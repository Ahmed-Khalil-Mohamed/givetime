# Visual assets

`public/brand-scene.png` is an AI-generated editorial illustration, not a photograph of actual customers or a product screenshot. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool.

Prompt:

Create a wide 16:9 natural documentary photograph for a community volunteering calendar website. Three volunteers' hands in practical gardening gloves gently planting a small leafy seedling together in a raised community garden bed, sunlit green leaves and warm earth. Crop to hands and plant, no faces or identifiable people, realistic horticultural tools, authentic candid moment, muted sage and terracotta palette. No text, no logos, no interface. Warm, credible community participation, no staged corporate handshake.

Brand icons are derived from the existing MIT-licensed Phosphor icon set; editable SVG sources and raster Windows icons are included in `public`.
